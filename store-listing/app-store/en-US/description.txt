SereneFlow turns your daily rituals into calm, guided routines. Build a sequence of timed steps (a morning stretch, a focus block, a tea break, a wind-down before bed) and let the app carry you from one step to the next, so you can stop watching the clock.

FIVE LIVING, ANIMATED TIMERS
• Serene Water drains gently as the step runs out
• Living Bonsai grows and blossoms, petals drifting in the wind
• Celestial Orbit circles a glowing core with drifting stardust
• Breathing Lotus opens and closes to a calm 4-4-6-2 breathing rhythm
• Zen Hourglass pours a stream of glowing sand
• Big, clear countdown digits you can read from across the room

GUIDANCE YOU CAN HEAR
• A calm voice announces each step and what comes next
• Synthesized Tibetan singing bowl and soft wooden chimes
• Gentle reminders when 75%, 50%, 25% or 10% of a step is left
• A soft 3-2-1 chime before every step ends
• Choose voice and bowl, voice only, bowl only, chimes, or completely silent

ROUTINES YOUR WAY
• Add, reorder and time steps in seconds
• Pick a visual timer and a sound style for each routine
• Duplicate a routine to make a variation
• Skip, go back, restart a step or pause at any time

STAYS WITH YOU
• Follow your routine on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island with a Live Activity
• Keeps guiding you with the screen off or while you use other apps

CALM BY DESIGN
• Four harmonized themes: Zen Sage, Ocean Mist, Sunset Clay and Midnight Starlight
• Keeps the screen awake while a routine runs, if you like
• Made for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Your routines are stored only on your device
• Works fully offline

Breathe, begin, and let the routine flow.
